FAQ
homefaq-labelSection LabelGood to Know
homefaq-h2H2Frequently Asked Questions
homefaq-q1FAQ QDo I need to be a certified diver to come?
homefaq-a1FAQ ANo. Base One is built for serious divers, but Cala Gonone is also a stunning place to visit — non-divers can snorkel, kayak, hike, climb, and explore while their group dives.
homefaq-q2FAQ QWhat level of diver is Base One for?
homefaq-a2FAQ AEveryone from recreational divers through to advanced cave and CCR. We run GUE training from Fundamentals upward, plus guided cave, reef, and open-water dives.
homefaq-q3FAQ QWhen is the diving season?
homefaq-a3FAQ AMost diving and courses run April through November, with warm, clear water and long bottom times. Off-season diving is possible on request.
homefaq-q4FAQ QCan you help with accommodation and transfers?
homefaq-a4FAQ AYes. We work with local hotels and apartments and can arrange airport shuttle transfers — you will not need a car in Cala Gonone.
homefaq-q5FAQ QHow do I get a price?
homefaq-a5FAQ AUse the Cost Estimates page to tell us what you want to do, and we will build a tailored plan for diving, training, and your stay.

Intro
divingintro-labelSection LabelThe Environment
divingintro-h2H2One of the last wild coastlines in the Mediterranean.
divingintro-leadLeadThe Gulf of Orosei and its forty kilometres of coastline are one of the last areas of the Italian coast without buildings or roads. High white limestone cliffs drop into turquoise water, hiding hundreds of dry and flooded caves — some of the longest underwater cave systems in Europe.
Caves
divingcave-1-nameCave NameGrotta del Bue Marino
divingcave-1-accessAccessBoat access · All levels
divingcave-1-descDescriptionThe most extensive system in the Gulf. Branching passages — Ramo Nord, Ramo Sud, and Ramo del Bue — span kilometres of mapped and unexplored development. Rich speleothems, a prominent halocline, and the long open galleries that made Orosei famous worldwide.
divingcave-2-nameCave NameBel Torrente
divingcave-2-accessAccessBoat access · Intermediate
divingcave-2-descDescriptionA powerful freshwater spring with a wide, photogenic entrance. Strong current near the resurgence gives way to calmer phreatic galleries deeper inside. Excellent visibility and rich decoration throughout.
divingcave-3-nameCave NameRisorgenza di Cala Luna
divingcave-3-accessAccessBoat access · Intermediate
divingcave-3-descDescriptionAccessed by boat into the bay of Cala Luna. Multiple sumps with vertical shafts and a striking halocline. Spectacular limestone formations and exceptional surroundings both above and below the surface.
divingcave-4-nameCave NameUtopia / Euforia
divingcave-4-accessAccessBoat access · Advanced
divingcave-4-descDescriptionTwin adjacent systems offering varied morphology and the option of a combined dive profile. Well-decorated passages and excellent photographic potential.
divingcave-5-nameCave NameGrotta del Fico
divingcave-5-accessAccessBoat access · Technical
divingcave-5-descDescriptionA southern system with dramatic vertical shafts and exceptionally rich formations. Greater depth and complexity make it best suited to experienced technical and rebreather divers. An additional site access fee applies.
divingcave-6-nameCave NameMaseduoe
divingcave-6-accessAccessBoat access · Advanced
divingcave-6-descDescriptionA quieter and less-visited system that rewards divers who take the time to explore beyond the main passage. A favourite among those who prefer caves without crowds.
